## Literal diff: brochure vs. production copy (verification pass, 2026-08-19)

**Unit I** — brochure lists 11 items in one flowing dash-separated line under the unit heading
"Basics of Direct Taxes & Indirect Taxes under Indian Constitution": Constitutional basics of
Taxation, Annual Financial Statement, Finance Act, Appropriation Act, Consolidated Funds of
India, Public Account of India, Comptroller and Auditor General of India, Finance Commission
of India, Centre-State Financial Relations under Indian Constitution, GST Council, 101
Constitutional amendment Act 2016. All 11 are present on the production page. Three notes,
none requiring a UI change (corrections to brochure typos, not additions beyond brochure
scope):
- Production says "101**st** Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016" — brochure omits the ordinal
  ("101 Constitutional amendment Act 2016"). The amendment's actual name includes "101st";
  this is a typo correction, not an invented fact.
- Production says "Consolidated Fund of India" (singular) — brochure says "Consolidated
  **Funds** of India" (plural). "Consolidated Fund of India" (Article 266) is the correct
  constitutional term; kept as-is.
- Production includes two topic chips, "Basics of Direct Taxes" and "Basics of Indirect
  Taxes", which are not separately itemized in the brochure's dash-separated body text — they
  are the unit's own title ("Basics of Direct Taxes & Indirect Taxes under Indian
  Constitution"). Kept as-is: true and central to the unit's own scope, not a fabrication.

**Unit II** ("Tax-related Adjudications", brochure page 8) — brochure lists 7 items: disputes
re: Assessment Officer (Income Tax Act and GST Acts), Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeal),
Commissioner of Customs, Excise and Service Tax (Appeal), Income Tax Appellate Tribunal,
Customs, Excise and Service Tax Appellate Tribunal, Writs and Writ Appeal before High Court
(tax matters), GST Appellate Tribunal. All 7 are present on the production page (as
"Assessment-Level Matters", "Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeal)", "Customs, Excise &
Service Tax Appeals", "ITAT", "CESTAT", "High Court", "GST Appellate Tribunal"). One note:
production and the forum strip use "**Assessing** Officer" — the brochure itself says
"Assessment Officer", which is not the correct statutory term (Section 2(7A), Income Tax Act,
1961 defines "Assessing Officer"). Kept as the legally correct term; this is a correction, not
an unsupported addition.
